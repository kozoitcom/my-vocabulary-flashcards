# Original EIKEN Grade 3 practice picture cards

These 30 original SVG scene illustrations correspond to workbook `英検3級面接問題_30セット_150問`, IDs 3-001 through 3-030.

For each card, the left area depicts the action referenced by Question 2, and the right area depicts the objects/count referenced by Question 3. These are independently created practice illustrations; they are not official EIKEN materials. They may be used as public raw URLs in the Google Sheet and related learning app.
